How to play

The loop: design your restaurant, staff it, and watch your crew work.

  • Customers walk in through doors when you have a free table with chairs and a waiter on duty. Blocked doors turn them away instantly.
  • Waiters seat guests, take orders, deliver food, and clear dirty tables.
  • Chefs cook orders at stoves. Every chef needs a stove.
  • Janitors clean toilets, repair arcade machines, and pick up trash.
  • Energy: staff work ~4 hour shifts. Feed them snacks or let them rest. If everyone collapses, the restaurant closes.
  • Dishes: learn recipes in the Menu tab with ingredients, then level dishes 1-10 for more Gourmet Points per plate.
  • Ingredients: daily login streaks, the Market, your garden (level 6), and the wandering Gourmet King.
  • Design tab: click an item, then click a tile to place it. Move and Sell tools rearrange for free (sell-back is 1/3).
  • Levels: earn Gourmet Points per plate cleared. Level up for coins, bigger restaurants, more staff, and more menu slots.
  • Toilets are needed from level 8. Dirty toilets send customers away angry.
  • Holiday tab: seasonal decor and dishes change every month.
